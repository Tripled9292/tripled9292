## About

**Dodson Development** is a web and app development company based in Raleigh, NC, with 4+ years of experience helping businesses of all sizes build a strong digital presence. We cover strategy, design, and development, with weekly check-ins and ongoing support after launch.

## What we do

- **Web development:** custom websites built with React, Vue.js, Node.js, and PHP
- **Mobile apps:** native and cross-platform iOS and Android apps with Flutter
- **Design:** UI/UX for web and mobile, plus graphic design
- **Ecommerce:** WordPress and Shopify stores
- **Geospatial mapping:** GIS mapping and data visualization
